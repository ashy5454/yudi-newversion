We are looking for bold researchers and low-level engineers who want to fundamentally rewrite how machines represent reality.

Our Hiring Philosophy

We do not care about Leetcode. We do not care if you can invert a binary tree on a whiteboard. We are tackling foundational problems that require deep, cross-disciplinary thinking between neuroscience and silicon.

We care if you can read Kanerva's paper on Sparse Distributed Memory, understand the biological implications, and then write an aggressively optimized CUDA kernel to implement it efficiently. We evaluate candidates based on deployed research, GitHub portfolios, and the ability to think outside the Transformer paradigm.
