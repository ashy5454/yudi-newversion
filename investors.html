Investor Overview

What Yudi Labs Is
A research-led AI company building brain-native AI systems, products, and applied solutions. We treat research as our core engine, and our commercial viability is proven through custom enterprise deployments and proprietary SaaS tools.
Why Now?
Current AI is powerful, but questions remain around continual learning, compositional generalization, interpretability, memory, and cross-modal grounding. As dense models hit diminishing returns on raw scaling, Yudi Labs is pursuing parallel architectural directions that target these specific gaps.
Technical Thesis
The next frontier in AI may require new substrates: sparse representation, local predictive learning, recurrent state, and relational structure. Our CMP architecture is the testing ground for this thesis.

Evidence So Far

  • Working CMP research line with continuous iteration.
  • Architecture iterations (v1.5 to v1.9) demonstrating adaptability.
  • Semantic representation experiments mapped.
  • Predictive coding toy-scale validation completed.
  • Clear roadmap toward capability benchmarks.

What This Is Not

Clarity in our claims:

  • Not a claim that CMP replaces Transformers today.
  • Not a claim that we have AGI.
  • Not a bet that we beat backprop on ordinary next-token accuracy.
  • Not only a chatbot company.

Roadmap to Seed

  • Phase 1: Research validation and architecture stability (Current)
  • Phase 2: Capability benchmarks on continual learning and few-shot tasks
  • Phase 3: Product/prototype development (CTS Beta)
  • Phase 4: Formal research collaborations with academic institutions
  • Phase 5: Pre-seed/seed funding milestones
