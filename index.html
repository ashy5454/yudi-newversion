Beyond token-first AI.

Modern AI systems are powerful, but most still depend on tokenized inputs, large-scale backpropagation, and attention-heavy processing. Yudi Labs studies alternative paths inspired by sparse neural activity, predictive coding, recurrent state, relational binding, and memory.

Current research programs

Our research is organized into programs and projects. CMP is one active project inside Yudi Labs research, not the whole company.

CMP
