Biological Constraints

Neuroscience Foundations at Yudi Labs.

Yudi AI Labs does not claim to replicate the brain. We use neuroscience as a source of constraints and inspiration for Yudi AI's architecture: sparse activity, predictive processing, recurrence, memory, local learning, and relational binding.

Sparse Coding

How biological networks represent complex concepts through highly specific, distributed patterns rather than dense, continuous blobs.

  • Sparse distributed representations
  • Sparse activity
  • Pattern overlap
  • Interpretability potential

Predictive Processing

The theory that the brain is fundamentally a prediction engine, constantly minimizing the delta between expectation and sensory reality.

  • Prediction error
  • Top-down and bottom-up signals
  • Local learning inspiration

Working Memory and Recurrence

How biological intelligence maintains persistent state over time, allowing for reasoning across temporal gaps.

  • State over time
  • Gated recurrence
  • Memory update and forgetting

Relational Binding

The ability to dynamically map entities to roles, allowing for compositionality and structural understanding.

  • Binding entities, roles, and relations
  • Structured internal representations

What We Are Not Claiming

  • We are not claiming that CMP replicates cortical circuits.
  • We are not claiming biological accuracy.
  • We are using neuroscience to guide engineering hypotheses.
