FAQ

Answers to common architectural, technical, and deployment questions.

What exactly does "brain-native" mean?
"Brain-native" means we do not rely on dense matrices predicting static tokens like traditional LLMs. Instead, we use sparse, neuromorphic activations that learn continuously via prediction error, mimicking human cortical networks. We process information experientially—mapping conceptual nodes directly to emotional weights and relational edges.
Is YUDI an LLM?
No. Traditional Large Language Models (LLMs) are statistical models based on the Transformer architecture. Our core thesis is the LCoM (Large Conversational Model). While LLMs process static text and forget it, LCoMs utilize our CMP (Conversational Meaning Patterns) architecture to maintain persistent, unbounded conversational states over long horizons.
How does your approach differ from standard RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)?
Standard RAG breaks documents into arbitrary chunks and uses basic cosine similarity to find overlapping text, which destroys critical semantic nuance. Our architecture, specifically deployed in our NARA agent, builds a sparse relational map of the concepts within a document. This allows us to synthesize multi-document hypotheses and provide exact, bi-directional citation grounding without semantic flattening.
How does CTS achieve an 80% cost reduction?
If you build a chatbot using a standard commercial API, you must send the entire conversational history back to the model for every single turn, creating an exponentially rising token cost. CTS acts as middleware. It dynamically compresses the conversational state into dense semantic representations, passing only the exact delta context required for the current interaction, flattening the cost curve from quadratic to linear.
Is the architecture production-ready?
Our infrastructural top-layer, CTS, is currently in closed beta and stable for production deployments with major LLM APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic). The underlying, fully tokenless CMP architecture and our specialized LCoM agents (NARA, Sam) are currently in closed Enterprise Pilots as we complete Phase 4 ablation studies and latency optimizations.
