Yudi AI Labs Research Collaboration

Yudi AI Labs is open to collaboration with neuroscientists, cognitive scientists, ML researchers, independent researchers, and academic labs working on sparse coding, predictive coding, memory, representation learning, cognitive architectures, and human-AI interaction.

Academic Labs & Universities

We partner with academic institutions to provide engineering support, compute access, or joint publication tracks for research aligned with brain-native architectures.

Neuroscientists & Cognitive Scientists

If you study human working memory, relational binding, or predictive processing in biological systems, we want to translate those constraints into actionable AI architecture.

Independent ML Researchers

We support independent researchers who have strong intuitions about alternatives to global backpropagation, dense embeddings, or token-first paradigms.

Fellow Frontier Labs

We are open to cross-lab collaboration on open benchmarks specifically targeting continual learning, cross-modal grounding, and few-shot relational reasoning.
