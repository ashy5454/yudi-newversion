About Yudi Labs

We exist to explore alternatives to the dominant token-first AI paradigm. We believe that true generalization and continual learning will require architectures constrained by biological principles: sparse representation, predictive processing, and relational memory.

However, we are not purely an academic institution. We believe that the best research must be grounded in real-world utility. This is why Yudi Labs operates a tripartite structure: theoretical research, owned product engineering, and custom solutions architecture for businesses.

We are a global team, rooted in India, building the foundational components for the next generation of artificial intelligence.
