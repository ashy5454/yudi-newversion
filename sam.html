SAM by Yudi AI

An advanced creative agent bridging persistent state-tracking with fluent, emotionally-aware generative synthesis.

The Emotion-Aware Stack

Most language models generate text that feels undeniably "AI". It is overly formal, mathematically average, and entirely devoid of emotional resonance. This occurs because Transformers have no native understanding of sentiment; they only predict the most statistically probable string of words.

Sam sits on top of Yudi AI's native LCoM infrastructure, meaning it inherently maps structural nodes to emotional weights. When Sam generates a long-form creative piece, it isn't just generating text; it is modulating its output based on a persistent internal emotional state that reacts to your prompts.

Core Capabilities

Hyper-Personalized Tone

Sam learns the exact cadence, formatting, and emotional flavor of your brand voice, applying it consistently across thousands of generations without context-drift.

Long-Horizon Continuity

Because Sam utilizes our CTS (Conversational Token System) internally, it can maintain the plot, character arcs, or thematic consistency of a 100-page document perfectly.

Dynamic Empathy

Ideal for customer-facing applications, Sam detects user frustration, joy, or urgency and dynamically adjusts its conversational output to match the situation.

Multi-Modal Synthesis

Sam is currently being tested with visual-structural data to generate contextually aware layout and design suggestions alongside text.

Sam is currently in closed alpha testing for enterprise content teams and creative agencies.
