Researching brain-native AI at Yudi Labs.

Yudi AI Labs studies how artificial intelligence can move beyond token-first prediction toward architectures shaped by sparse representation, predictive coding, recurrent state, memory, and Conversational Meaning Patterns (CMP).

Why Brain-Native AI?

Brain-native AI does not mean copying the brain directly. It means using principles from biological intelligence — sparse activity, local learning, predictive processing, recurrence, memory, and relational structure — as design constraints for new AI systems.
