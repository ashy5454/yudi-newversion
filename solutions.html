Persistent AI Companions

Current companions feel robotic because they reset context. Using our Conversational Token System (CTS) layered with Sam, developers can deploy companions that remember interactions from years ago without ballooning API costs.

The companion maps user interactions to emotional weights, allowing the agent to react with genuine empathy based on the historical relationship, not just the immediate prompt.

CTS + Sam Agent
Architecture Driver
Emotional Weighting
Business Outcome
80% reduction in long-horizon API costs.

Enterprise Knowledge Research

Standard RAG pipelines fail on complex, dense documents like legal contracts or clinical trial data due to semantic flattening. They retrieve fragments devoid of macro-context.

Our NARA agent reads entire corpus sets and builds a sparse relational map. It can answer synthesis questions like "How do these 40 clinical trials contradict each other?" while providing exact, bi-directional citations to the source material.

Primary Product
NARA Agent
Architecture Driver
Sparse Relational Edges
Business Outcome
Zero hallucination in technical workflows.

Long-Horizon Co-Pilots

Coding and strategy co-pilots are currently limited to immediate tasks. They cannot hold the entire 6-month architectural roadmap of a software project in their context window.

LCoMs utilize Hippocampal-style memory consolidation. They push irrelevant details into dormant storage and only retrieve them when a partial cue is detected, allowing the co-pilot to assist on massive, multi-month projects with perfect contextual awareness.

Primary Product
Enterprise Pilot
Architecture Driver
Gated Working Memory
Business Outcome
Infinite context scaling.
